When a Wind Turbine Goes Dark, How Much Energy Was Lost?

Turning missing blackout data into an auditable Business Interruption insurance claim.

🔌

1. The Grid Outage

Cable damage or grid faults disconnect the wind farm. The turbines stop generating electricity and revenue drops to zero.

🌫️

2. The Sensor Blind Spot

Normally, we compute lost energy from the turbine's own wind sensor. During a blackout, that sensor is offline too (NaN).

🧭

3. Our Automated Solution

We learn how neighbouring wind parks (Park O & Park H) relate to our turbine before the outage, reconstruct the missing wind, and calculate lost MWh.

Slide 2 · Explainable Architecture BayWa r.e. Wind Challenge

Why Our Model Is Simple, Physical & 100% Explainable

Prioritizing explainability — one clean equation instead of a black box.

Predicted Wind Speed = w₀ + w_O · Speed(Park O) + w_H · Speed(Park H) + Direction Adjustments (u, v)
🔍

One Readable Formula

  • Uses Bayesian Ridge Regression.
  • Every turbine gets a single linear equation you can check on a calculator.
  • No hidden layers or opaque decision rules.
🏞️

Why Aggregate into Parks?

  • Turbines in the same park (O1–O2 or H1–H5) feel >96% identical wind.
  • Giving each turbine its own weight makes them fight and creates fake negative weights.
  • Park medians give clean ~40% / ~40% weights and survive single-sensor dropouts.
🏆

Better Than Other Models

  • vs. LightGBM (450 Trees): Black box that fails seasonal shifts (-20% error) and can't be audited.
  • vs. Raw OLS Regression: Suffers from negative collinear weights.
  • Explainability is key for insurance sign-off.
💡 Example (Turbine T2): v_T2 = 0.61 + 0.40·Speed_O + 0.41·Speed_H − 0.04·u_O + 0.04·v_O + 0.01·u_H − 0.08·v_H 100% Transparent
Slide 3 · Native Uncertainty Quantification BayWa r.e. Wind Challenge

Native Bayesian Uncertainty: Two Bands, Zero Crossing

Uncertainty comes directly from probability math — not ad-hoc quantile trees.

🎯

Why Native Bayesian Beats Quantile Estimates

  • Quantile Models (e.g. LightGBM): Train 3 separate models for P10, P50, and P90. Because they don't share math, curves can cross (P10 > P50) and require manual patching.
  • Native Bayesian Ridge: Predicts a complete bell curve (mean μ & spread σ) at every 10-minute step.
  • Guaranteed Consistency: Percentiles (50%–99% slider in our app) never cross and separate formula certainty from wind gusts.
📊

Two Uncertainties Separated Automatically

  • 1. 95% Coefficient CI (Dark Inner Band — ±2.0 MWh):
    "How sure are we about our formula?"
    With 12,400+ training intervals, the formula weights are pinned down extremely tightly.
  • 2. 80% Predictive Band P10–P90 (Light Outer Band):
    "How gusty is a single 10-minute step?"
    Over a 3-day outage, random 10-minute gusts cancel out — so true energy stays close to P50.
